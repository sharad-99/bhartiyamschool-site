Boarding @ BVN

Bhartiyam Vidya Niketan is one of the best boarding schools in Madhya Pradesh. The school has an impressive infrastructure with dedicated staff to make sure that the boarding students get the complete advantage of being in a residential setting. Residential facilities is being provided from class 1st to 12th.

The school has a separate boarding for boys and girls. For students, the boarding is a home away from home. Accommodation for students is provided in well maintained, fully furnished, air conditioned spacious rooms.

We have insured that the boarding is clean, comfortable and safe and within easy reach of all activities. The boarding students are encouraged to take full advantage of all the facilities and opportunities provided to them. It’s here, that the young students learn the spirit of togetherness and imbibe the value of sharing and caring for one another.

There are security guards stationed at all entry points as well as constant surveillance by CCTV cameras to monitor the students’ safety.

The specially designed schedule for the students attempts to harmonize the physical, intellectual and moral growth of each student. While an effective tutorial system reinforce the academic/pastoral needs of the students, there is a highly structured academic monitoring specially for the boarding students by a Hostel Co-ordinator who looks after the academic development and monitors progress of each pupil. The students well-being is looked after by a team of dedicated house staff and wardens, who provide them with a homely and friendly atmosphere.
