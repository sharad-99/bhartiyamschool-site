Guru Purnima

A grand celebration was organized with enthusiasm at Bharatiyam Vidya Niketan on the occasion of Guru Purnima. The program commenced with Guru Vandana (salutations to the teacher). Students adorned the traditional tilak (mark on the forehead) and offered auspicious fruits to seek blessings from their gurus. The relationship between the guru (teacher) and shishya (student) was beautifully portrayed through a street play (nukkad natak). The principal, Mrs. Shikha Gaur, highlighted the importance of the guru in students' lives during her address, emphasizing that a student climbs the ladder of success through the knowledge imparted by the guru. The existence of gurus is considered inseparable from the creation of the earth. On this occasion, the school administrator, Mr. Umesh Mishra, also expressed his thoughts on the greatness of gurus and blessed the students. The program was coordinated by Mrs. Pallavi Dubey and Mr. Sunil Gupta, who were present throughout the event.

NEP Workshop

Bhartiyam Vidya Niketan organized a one-day workshop for the faculty members of different schools of Gwalior on New National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 in collaboration with CBSE on Sunday 23/7/23. The main objective of this Workshop was to create awareness about the National Education Policy-2020 amongst teachers and to familiarize them with the skills to help them understand its nuances, curriculum changes, the shift in pedagogical skills and its implementation for the holistic development of its stakeholders, the students. The session was conducted by Mr.Rajesh Sharma ( Principal of Sardar Patel Public school) and Mr.Abbas Ali Barnagarwala ( Columbia Convent,Indore)

Plantation Drive

Bhartiyam Vidya Niketan School Joins Hands with EPCO and Social Forestry Unit, Gwalior for Grand Plantation Drive Gwalior, September 16, 2023 - Bhartiyam Vidya Niketan School in collaboration with the Environmental Planning and Coordination Organization (EPCO) and the Social Forestry Unit, came together today for a remarkable plantation drive, setting a green example for the community. The event took place on September 16, 2023, and was graced by the presence of Mr. TS Suliya, Chief Conservator of Forest, Gwalior. Mr. Suliya's participation in the plantation drive was not just symbolic; he led by example by planting a fruit tree himself. During the event, he also took the opportunity to impart valuable knowledge to the students of Bhartiyam Vidya Niketan on the importance of collecting seeds and their proper dispersal in the forest ecosystem. At the end of the program, Mr. Gautam Bhagchandani, Chairman of Bhartiyam Vidya Niketan, honored Mr. Suliya with a memento as a token of appreciation for his invaluable contribution to environmental conservation.
